How students use it

A sharper prep loop from first question to final mock.

Daily workflow

  • Start on the dashboard and identify the weakest area by recent timing and accuracy.
  • Run a topic paper to isolate the problem and build confidence under controlled difficulty.
  • Review the mistake bank, then finish with random questions to test transfer.

Exam-week workflow

  • Alternate full mock papers with focused recovery sets between mock days.
  • Use analytics to compare pacing drift across Verbal Reasoning, Decision Making and Quantitative Reasoning.
  • Keep Situational Judgement review visible without letting it crowd out cognitive score work.
